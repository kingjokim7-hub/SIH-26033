## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose developing FarmDirect, an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers and bulk buyers directly.

The platform reduces dependency on multiple intermediaries by allowing farmers to list their agricultural products, set prices and receive orders directly from buyers.

Key features:
- Direct farmer-to-consumer and farmer-to-bulk-buyer connections.
- AI-based demand forecasting to predict the demand for agricultural products.
- Smart logistics support for transportation and delivery.
- Route optimization to reduce delivery time and transportation costs.
- Real-time market price updates.
- Transparent pricing and digital payment facilities.
Innovation and uniqueness:

Unlike traditional agricultural marketplaces, FarmDirect combines direct selling, AI-based demand prediction and intelligent logistics in a single platform.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:
- The application can be developed using affordable and widely available technologies.
- Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones or computers.
- Existing mapping and payment services can support logistics and transactions.
- The system can initially be implemented in a small geographical area and expanded gradually.
Potential challenges:
1. Limited digital awareness among some farmers.
2. Transportation and delivery management.
3. Maintaining product quality during transportation.
4. Fluctuations in agricultural prices and demand.
5. Building trust between farmers and consumers.
Strategies to overcome challenges:
- Provide a simple, multilingual and user-friendly interface.
- Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.
- Introduce product quality verification.
- Update market prices regularly.
- Implement user ratings and transparent order tracking.
